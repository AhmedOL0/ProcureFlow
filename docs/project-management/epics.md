# Epics (Jira-ready)

Import each section as one Epic; stories live in `initial-backlog.md`.
Naming convention: `PF-<epic#>-<story#>`.

1. **Identity & Access** — login, JWT + refresh rotation, RBAC, tenant sessions.
2. **Organization Management** — tenants, departments, memberships, invitations.
3. **Supplier Management** — suppliers, contacts, categories, performance.
4. **Procurement** — purchase requests, items, lifecycle, idempotent submit.
5. **Approval Workflow** — workflows, steps, decisions, delegation, escalation.
6. **Budget Management** — budgets, allocations, in-transaction reservation.
7. **Purchase Orders** — orders from approved requests, supplier linkage.
8. **Invoicing** — invoices, 3-way match lite, payment status.
9. **Notifications** — event-driven email + in-app, preferences.
10. **Analytics** — procurement/supplier KPIs, spend dashboards.
11. **AI Procurement Copilot** — Groq adapter, copilot, extraction, quotation intel.
12. **Audit & Compliance** — append-only audit trail, admin visibility.
13. **DevOps & Observability** — pipelines, containers, metrics, Terraform.
14. **Quality & Testing** — coverage floors, E2E journeys, performance budgets.
